# Troubleshooting

For the physical proof, ensure the iGrill is powered, close the official Weber application, insert
at least one probe and remain within Bluetooth range. Do not paste private addresses or secrets into
issues. Preserve the redacted program output and the matching on-device display temperature.

The proof distinguishes discovery, connection, V202 service validation, authentication, unit,
battery and per-probe read failures. BlueZ or pairing changes will only be considered after capturing
the first safe failure from the target environment.
